📜 Policy & Decarbonization Roadmap for Dr. Sarah Williams
  • ▸
    Commercial Fleet Decarbonization Multiplier: One electric transit bus abates 45+ tons of CO2 annually — equivalent to taking 22 passenger cars off the road. Municipal transit subsidies yield the highest carbon ROI.
  • ▸
    Cold Climate Resilience: Sodium-ion batteries retain 85%+ capacity at -20°C compared to 55% for NMC/LFP, making them key for Nordic and Northern North American adoption without range degradation.
  • ▸
    Closed-Loop Battery Recycling: Mandating 95%+ recovery of Nickel, Cobalt, and Lithium by 2030 will decouple manufacturing expansion from raw virgin mining bottlenecks.
